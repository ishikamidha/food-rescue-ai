# FoodRescue AI 🥗🚚
> **Dynamic Expiry & Route-Optimized Food Rescue Network**  
> *Developed for Engage 6.0 – Code with AI Hackathon (The NorthCap University & InfraOne School of AI)*  
> **Theme:** Food for Everyone (Theme 06)

---

## 📌 Problem Statement
Traditional food rescue programs frequently fail due to **logistical bottlenecks** rather than a lack of donations. Edible cooked food and fresh produce spoil in transit because existing distribution networks lack real-time freshness/shelf-life estimation and dynamic route matching based on remaining safe consumption hours.

---

## 💡 Solution Overview
**FoodRescue AI** bridges the gap between food donors (canteens, restaurants, event organizers) and local beneficiaries (shelters, food banks, community centers) by combining **Computer Vision AI** with **Dynamic Route Optimization**:

1. **📷 AI Quality & Expiry Check:** Computer vision models analyze food images to instantly assess freshness scores (0–100%), estimate safe consumption shelf-life, and detect food items.
2. **🗺️ Dynamic Route-Optimized Matching:** Graph-based algorithms match donors with the nearest available volunteers and shelters based on transit times versus remaining safe consumption hours.
3. **📊 Real-Time Impact Dashboard:** Live metrics tracking meals saved, CO₂ emissions prevented, and average pickup velocity.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & AI Integration

* **Frontend:** React, Next.js, Tailwind CSS, Lucide Icons
* **Generative & Vision AI:** Google Gemini / OpenAI Vision API (Computer Vision Freshness Assessment)
* **Prototyping & AI Acceleration:** Bolt.new, Cursor AI
* **Design & Pitch:** Gamma.app

---

## 🚀 Key Features

* **Donor Portal:** Instant drag-and-drop food image scanning with quick sample presets.
* **Logistics & Route Map:** Real-time driver status tracking, route matching, and transit time indicators.
* **Impact Analytics Hub:** Real-time metrics highlighting saved food value (₹), methane diversion (kg CO₂), and total meals delivered.
